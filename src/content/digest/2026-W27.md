## One Thing I'm Watching

The Sonnet 5 tokenizer caveat. The 1.0–1.35x token expansion on input means the headline $2/MTok price narrows on real enterprise workloads — large documents, long system prompts, RAG pipelines. Anthropic called it "roughly cost-neutral" at launch. Enterprise teams on high-volume agentic workloads should benchmark their actual payloads before updating their cost models. If the effective price is $2.50–2.70 on tokenizer-heavy content, that changes the calculus on Sonnet 5 vs Opus 4.8 for specific workload types.
