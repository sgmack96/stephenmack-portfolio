## The Big Picture

5 entries this week, and the densest week of the digest so far. The week opened with Sun Valley putting every AI CEO in the same room while retail bled on semis. By Wednesday, Iran had replaced semiconductors as the market's primary variable. By Friday, GPT-5.6 launched a three-tier model family, SK Hynix's $26.5B IPO validated the AI infrastructure thesis at the institutional layer, and a four-paragraph blog post about LLMs absorbing your bad code patterns turned out to be the most practically useful piece of writing all week.

The thread across all five entries: the gap between what's true and what's priced. Samsung's 19x profit surge is true. The 200-350% semis run was priced wrong. Anthropic's J-space paper is a genuine interpretability breakthrough. The safety implications will take years to price correctly. The "LLMs are sponges" insight is true for every team using AI to write code. Almost nobody has priced it into their engineering workflow yet.
