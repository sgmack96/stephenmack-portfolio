## The Big Picture

5 entries this week. The Fable ban was the center of gravity — everything else orbited it.

SpaceX acquired Cursor for $60B in stock-not-cash, using an artificially inflated float to buy the product and distribution that xAI couldn't build fast enough. The Entity List freeze revealed that the US government isn't maintaining its own export control infrastructure — 100+ companies approved for blacklisting, none published in 8 months. The Fed signaled rate hikes as Iran war inflation ran hot, and the US-Iran MOU turned out to be a 60-day clock, not a settlement. AWS moved to sell Trainium chips to third parties but hit the TSMC bottleneck that constrains everyone.

And running through all of it: the question of model lock-in. The Fable ban showed that building on a single model provider is infrastructure dependency, not just a vendor preference. Chinese open models — GLM-5.2 launched the day after the ban — became the sovereign alternative you can't recall with an export control. Cloudflare's model-agnostic vulnerability harness kept running because it was designed to not care which model was underneath. The companies that built for portability from day one survived the week without breaking a sweat.

On the build side, SE Intel closed the multi-tenancy stack: three layers (RAG, memory, audit), three deterministic probes, three `isolationOk: true`. The end-to-end test and Blog #1 wrapped Week 1.

## One Thing I'm Watching

Chinese open model acceleration. GLM-5.2 launched the day after the Fable ban, explicitly citing US unreliability. It's open weight — once released, it can't be recalled by export controls. Microsoft is reportedly considering DeepSeek for Copilot Cowork to save on inference costs, despite being a major OpenAI shareholder. If the US proceeds with frontier model export controls, Chinese open models become the default alternative for every non-US government and enterprise. The irony: export controls designed to keep AI capabilities out of adversary hands may be accelerating the adoption of Chinese models globally. Worth watching whether that accelerates over the summer.
