## One Thing I'm Watching

The paired Anthropic/OpenAI economic research from Friday. Both labs published real usage data on the same day showing agentic AI is already transforming knowledge work — not as a forecast, but as a measurement. The counterintuitive finding that heavy automation correlates with career optimism rather than fear is the data point worth tracking over the next several months. If it holds as adoption widens beyond early adopters, it changes the policy and enterprise adoption conversation significantly.
